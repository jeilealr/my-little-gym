# Quick training reference — starter routines (Variation A)

**Training days and minutes are up to you.** The fourth exercise is optional if short on time. Weights are intentionally blank; choose a manageable load. Warm up before heavy sets. Equipment availability can vary at Thomaskirche.

## Chest A — Machine-friendly

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Chest press machine | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Incline dumbbell press | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Pec deck fly | 2 × 12–15 | 75 s |
| Cable chest fly (optional) | 2 × 12–15 | 60 s |

## Legs A — Glutes & Lower Body

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Leg press machine | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Hip thrust / glute drive machine | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 | 120 s |
| Seated leg curl (optional) | 2 × 10–15 | 75 s |

## Back A — Pulldown and rows

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lat pulldown | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Chest-supported machine row | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Seated cable row | 2 × 10–12 | 75 s |
| Reverse pec deck / rear delt fly (optional) | 2 × 12–15 | 60 s |

## Arms Shoulders A — Balanced arms and shoulders

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Shoulder press machine | 3 × 8–12 | 105 s |
| Cable triceps pushdown (bar) | 3 × 10–12 | 75 s |
| Dumbbell hammer curl | 3 × 10–12 | 75 s |
| Dumbbell lateral raise (optional) | 2 × 12–15 | 60 s |

**Progression:** Once you can complete all working sets at the top of the repetition range with good technique in multiple sessions, consider adding the smallest sensible weight increment. Stop a painful movement.

**Dumbbell entries:** Record the weight of *one dumbbell* where the exercise says “per dumbbell,” not the sum.
