Checked 28 October 2026.

## Gym and location

- **Confirmed location**: Fitness First Leipzig Thomaskirche, Petersstraße 20, 04109 Leipzig, Germany.
- **Official club page**: https://www.fitnessfirst.de/clubs/leipzig-thomaskirche
- **Club page verifies**: Performance Area (modern power racks, strength machines and free weights), EGYM equipment, functional training, Booty Zone / Glutebuilder, cardio facilities. These are **area-level claims**, not an inventory of exact machines.
- **Important source caveat**: Some Fitness First support pages that list dozens of specific machines look general but belong to entirely different clubs. For example, https://support.fitnessfirst.de/hc/de/articles/38199447009693-Welche-Ger%C3%A4te-habt-ihr-im-Studio belongs to the support section whose location article gives **Jägerstraße 27, 10117 Berlin** (https://support.fitnessfirst.de/hc/de/articles/37984191670557-Wo-befindet-sich-das-Studio). **Do not use that machine list as evidence for Leipzig.** All Leipzig individual machines remain unverified in this package.

## Training evidence and implementation interpretation

- **ACSM (17 March 2026)**: https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/ . Prioritize consistency and individualized training. Updated guidance supports all major muscle groups roughly twice weekly, while favoring simple sustainable exercise selection over unnecessary complexity; muscle gain can benefit from around 10 weekly sets/muscle group.
- **Primary study/position stand**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/
- **NHS general strength training guidance**: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/how-to-improve-strength-flexibility/
- **NASM exercise guide**: https://www.nasm.org/workout-exercise-guidance
- **Mayo Clinic leg press**: https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/multimedia/leg-press/vid-20084684
- **ACE exercise library**: https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/

The four-way chest/legs/back/arms split is a *user-selected starting preference* and may not be optimal when used as only two workouts per week. Do not interpret the presence of four templates as a mandatory weekly calendar. For lower frequency, offer a separate full-body / upper-lower category later and explain why.

## Exercise image licensing

Potential source: https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset

Official free-tier licensing: https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset/blob/main/LICENSE-DATA.md

- Permits **in-app use** of free-tier images/data for personal or commercial use with visible attribution.
- Requires attribution linked visibly, e.g. `Exercise data by RepDB (repdb.co)` and https://repdb.co .
- **Does not permit repackaging/redistributing** the dataset as a separate dataset or a derivative dataset.
- **Does not permit feeding their images into generative image tools**, including to restyle them.
- Premium-tier preview assets are not licensed for production use.
- This ZIP contains **none** of their JSON, images, animations or other distributed data. All workout and movement text was newly authored for this package, and illustrations are intentionally pending.
